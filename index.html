The Resilient Path

Book

Managing Life with Chronic Pain · Pendleton B. Wickersham, MD, and Louise Locker, LCSW

The Resilient Path redefines the experience of chronic pain by shifting the focus from getting rid of symptoms to living well through the cultivation of deep physical and emotional resilience. Grounded in the biopsychosocial model of pain, it draws on both clinical expertise and lived experience.

RheumCompanion

Available now

iOS and Android · Free

A trusted companion for understanding and managing rheumatologic conditions, designed with input from a board-certified rheumatologist. Evidence-based information drawn from FDA drug labels and PubMed research.

  • Disease encyclopedia — 13 conditions including RA, lupus, psoriatic arthritis, ankylosing spondylitis, gout, Sjögren’s, and vasculitis
  • Medication reference — 7 drug classes with FDA package insert information
  • RheumBot — AI-powered Q&A with sourced, plain-language answers
  • Drug interaction checker — reports grouped by severity with clinical explanations
  • Symptom & flare tracker — exportable 30-day reports for your care team
  • Clinical trial finder

SootheQuest

Available now

iOS and Android · Free

A match-3 puzzle game built from the ground up for people living with chronic illness and chronic pain — and for anyone who wants a game that respects their energy. Genuinely engaging puzzles, with every pressure mechanic deliberately removed.

  • A rest-friendly streak — it counts the days you show up, forgives rest days, and losing never breaks it
  • The Zen Garden — a no-fail sanctuary mode. No moves, no timer, no losing
  • Tiny Wins — one-minute levels for low-energy days that still count toward your daily goal
  • A gentle boost — struggling with a level? The game quietly offers a little extra room, no judgment
  • Real encouragement — a line from The Resilient Path after each level, saved to your Comfort Journal
  • The Calm Corner — guided box breathing with rain and wave soundscapes
  • Comfort & Access — larger gems and tap-only controls for steady hands
