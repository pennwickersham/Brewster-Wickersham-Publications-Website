Would you leave a review?

If the book, the workbook, or one of the apps has been useful to you, a sentence or two is what helps the next person with chronic pain find it.

Honest is far more useful than glowing. If something did not work for you, that is worth writing too.

Before you do

A few honest words

Reviews are optional and never rewarded

Nothing is offered in exchange for a review — no discount, no free copy, no consideration of any kind. Amazon and both app stores prohibit incentivized reviews, and a review bought that way is worthless to the person reading it.

If you are a patient of Dr. Wickersham: these books and apps are not part of your treatment plan, and whether you review them, ignore them, or criticize them will never affect your care in any way.
