Would you leave a review?

If the book, the workbook, or one of the apps has been useful to you, a sentence or two is what helps the next person with chronic pain find it.

Honest is far more useful than glowing. If something did not work for you, that is worth writing too.

App Store & Google Play

The apps

Both stores only accept reviews from inside their own app, on a phone or tablet signed in to the account that installed it. There is no review box on either store’s website — so if a button below lands you on a plain product page, you are on a computer, not doing anything wrong.

You are reading this on a computer

Neither store will let you write a review here. Open this page on the phone or tablet the app is installed on, and the buttons below will take you straight into the store app.

brewsterwickershampublications.com/review

  2. Android: the Google Play button opens the listing in the Play Store app. Scroll past the screenshots to Ratings and reviews, tap the stars under Rate this app, then Write a review.

Before you do

A few honest words

Reviews are optional and never rewarded

Nothing is offered in exchange for a review — no discount, no free copy, no consideration of any kind. Amazon and both app stores prohibit incentivized reviews, and a review bought that way is worthless to the person reading it.

If you are a patient of Dr. Wickersham: these books and apps are not part of your treatment plan, and whether you review them, ignore them, or criticize them will never affect your care in any way.
