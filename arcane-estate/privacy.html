Privacy Policy

The Arcane Estate · Brewster Wickersham Publications · Effective 30 September 2026

The Arcane Estate is made by Brewster Wickersham Publications ("we"). This policy explains what information the game handles on Android, iPhone and iPad, and the web.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no account, no sign-in and no server of ours. We do not collect, receive or store any information about you.

What stays on your device

Your progress — the estate, your streak, your settings — is saved on your own device (in the app's storage on Android and iOS, and in your browser's storage on the web). It never leaves your device unless you choose to write a copy of it yourself from Settings → Your save. Deleting the app, or clearing the browser's site data, deletes it.

Daily reminders, if you turn them on, are scheduled by your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Third-party services (Android)

The Android version uses two services run by other companies. They act under their own privacy policies, and we do not receive the information they collect.

  • Google AdMob shows ads to players who have not removed them. AdMob may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and information about ad interactions and app performance, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first, and you can change that choice in Settings → Privacy choices. You can also reset or opt out of your advertising ID in your device's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • RevenueCat processes in-app purchases made through Google Play. It receives purchase records, a random app user identifier and basic device information so that your purchases can be confirmed and restored. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Paying patrons see no ads. The iPhone, iPad and web versions currently have no ads, no purchases and no third-party services, and collect nothing.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
